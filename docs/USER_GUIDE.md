## 1. What LinguaForge is
LinguaForge helps you learn a language with a personal study plan. It measures your level, then builds a daily plan that mixes lessons, exercises, flashcards, writing and conversation practice with an AI tutor.

**Languages you can learn:** English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Arabic and Chinese (Mandarin, simplified script).
**Interface languages:** English and Arabic (Arabic is shown right-to-left).

A few things to know from the start:
- Your progress, levels and statistics come only from what you actually do in the app. Nothing is made up.
- Scores from AI feedback and exam practice are **estimates**. They are not official certificates or exam results.
- Some features need a microphone (speaking, pronunciation, voice tutor) or depend on your plan (see [§17](#17-plans-and-billing)).

## 2. Create your account
1. Open the app and choose **Register**.
2. Enter your name, email and a password. Choose your **native language**, your **interface language** (English or Arabic) and your **time zone**. The time zone decides when your day, streak and daily limits reset.
3. You are signed in straight away and taken to onboarding.

**Signing in:** use **Login** with your email and password.
**Forgot your password?** On the login page choose **Forgot password**, enter your email and follow the link we send you to set a new one.

## 3. Set up your first language (onboarding)
Onboarding asks a few short questions. Your answers shape your plan, and you can change them later.

| Step | What you choose | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Language | The language to learn | Each language has its own plan and progress |
| Variety | e.g. a regional variant, where offered | Used for audio and regional vocabulary |
| Goal | General, career, travel, academic, immigration or exams | Decides which skills get the most time |
| Level (optional) | How you'd describe your level | Only a starting point; the placement test measures your real level |
| Time | Minutes per day, study days, preferred time | Sets the size and rhythm of your daily plan |
| Focus (optional) | Skills you especially want to improve | Those skills get extra weight |

When you finish, you are invited to take the placement test.

## 4. Take the placement test
The placement test finds your CEFR level (Pre-A1 to C2) for each skill.

- **How it works:** questions adapt to your answers, getting harder or easier. Answer without a dictionary; it's fine not to know.
- **Sections:** listening, reading, grammar and vocabulary are multiple choice. Writing and speaking sections are scored by AI and count towards your plan's monthly quota.
- **Skipping:** you can skip a section (for example speaking, if you have no microphone). Skipped sections show as *not assessed*, and your plan will use your self-reported level for them.
- **Finishing early:** you can finish at any time. Sections with too few answers are marked *not assessed*.
- **Results:** you get a level per skill, with a note when a result is provisional. Your overall level is not a simple average of the skills. Results also list strengths and topics to work on, which feed your plan.

You start (or continue) the placement from the card on **Home**. Afterwards, **View results** on Home opens your report again. When a new placement would help, it appears as an activity in your daily plan.

## 5. Your Home page and daily plan
**Home** is where you start each day. It shows:
- **Today's plan:** a list of activities sized to your daily minutes. Each one says what it is and why it was chosen, for example "weak topic from your placement" or "next lesson in your course". Tap an activity to start it.
- Your **streak**, today's **XP** and quick links to continue where you left off.

How the plan is built:
- Time is split between skills according to your goal, your focus skills and your levels.
- It picks the **next lesson** in your course, exercises on **weak grammar topics**, **flashcards that are due**, and writing or tutor practice.
- It adapts as you study. Topics you keep getting wrong come back; topics you master move to occasional review.

Studying in a **session** (from **My learning**) tracks your study time, which counts towards your streak and your progress charts.

## 6. Courses and lessons (My learning)
Open **My learning → Courses**. Each language has two courses:

| Course | Levels | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| **Foundations** | Pre-A1 → A2 | Introductions, everyday objects, routines, likes |
| **B1: everyday independence** | B1 → B2 | Telling stories about the past, opinions and advice, hypotheticals, longer texts |

The app suggests the right **next lesson** for your level. If you're already at B1, it starts you on the B1 course. You can still open any lesson yourself.

**Inside a lesson:**
1. Read the introduction and the **learning objectives**.
2. Study the **grammar explanation** and the **words**. Tap a word to hear it and see its meaning (Chinese words show pinyin).
3. Do the lesson's **exercises**.
4. Choose **Complete lesson** once all its exercises are done. The lesson's words are added to your flashcard deck automatically.

## 7. Practice
**Practice** lists exercises in your language, filtered by skill. Every answer is scored straight away, with explanations.

| Exercise type | What you do |
|---|---|
| Multiple choice | Pick the right answer |
| Fill in the gap (cloze) | Type the missing word |
| Sentence building (ordering) | Put the words in the right order |
| Reading | Read a text and answer questions; tap any word to translate it |
| Listening | Listen to a recording and answer questions |
| Dictation | Listen and type what you hear |
| Say it aloud (repeat) | Record yourself saying a sentence and get a pronunciation estimate |

Notes:
- Listening and dictation appear only once their audio has been generated.
- The pronunciation check is an *intelligibility estimate*. For Chinese it also flags likely **tone** mistakes. It is not a judgement of your accent.
- Results update your skill mastery, which feeds your plan.

## 8. Grammar
**Practice → Grammar topics** lists the grammar of your language by level. Each topic has an explanation with examples (in your interface language) and linked exercises. Your mastery of each topic is shown and is updated by your exercise results.

## 9. Writing
**Practice → Writing** offers writing tasks at your level, such as emails, short texts and essays.
1. Open a task, read the prompt and the target length (Chinese counts **characters**, not words).
2. Write and **submit**.
3. Within a short time you get **AI feedback**: an estimated level or band, comments on each criterion, and corrections with explanations.

Every submission is kept under **Submissions**, so you can compare your texts over time. Writing evaluations count towards your plan's monthly limit. If the evaluation fails on our side, it isn't charged.

## 10. The AI tutor
**AI tutor** lets you practise real conversations.
1. **Choose a situation**, e.g. ordering in a café, a job interview or small talk.
2. **Choose a mode:**
   - **Text:** type your messages.
   - **Voice (push to talk):** hold the button, speak, and release. Your speech is transcribed and the tutor answers aloud.
   - **Live voice:** a real-time spoken conversation. Your browser connects directly to the speech service with a short-lived key, and time is counted in blocks of minutes.
3. The tutor adapts to your level and shows **corrections** as you go.
4. Finish the conversation to get a **report** with your mistakes, useful phrases and suggestions. Conversations with at least four of your turns earn XP.

Tutor messages and live-voice minutes have daily or monthly limits depending on your plan.

## 11. Vocabulary and flashcards
**Vocabulary** is your personal word deck.
- Words are added when you complete lessons, when you tap words in readings, or from **Search the dictionary** on the Vocabulary page.
- **Review** shows the cards that are due. Rate how well you remembered each one. The app schedules the next review using spaced repetition (FSRS), so easy words come back less often and hard ones sooner.
- Each card shows the meaning, an example sentence, pronunciation (pinyin for Chinese) and related words where available.

Doing your due reviews every day is one of the most effective habits in the app.

## 12. Exam practice
**Practice → Exams** offers timed practice tests **in the format of** recognised exams:

| Exam format | Language | Sections |
|---|---|---|
| IELTS Academic | English | Listening, reading, writing, speaking (estimated bands) |
| DELE A2 | Spanish | Reading, listening, writing, speaking (estimated readiness) |
| DELF A2 | French | Listening, reading, writing, speaking (estimated readiness) |
| Goethe-Zertifikat A2 | German | Reading, listening, writing, speaking (estimated readiness) |
| HSK 1 | Chinese | Listening, reading (estimated readiness) |

- **The timer is real.** It is kept on our server, so closing the tab does not stop it. Answers after the time limit are not accepted. You can change answers until you submit a section.
- **Listening** recordings play during the test. Scripts are shown only in the review afterwards.
- **Results:** an estimated band (IELTS) or an estimated chance of passing (A1/A2 exams), each with a range. All material is original LinguaForge content. **We are not affiliated with the exam providers, and results are not official scores.**
- **Retakes:** each exam has more than one practice test. A new attempt gives you the one you've taken least, and your history shows which test (1, 2, …) you took. An unfinished attempt is resumed instead.
- Practice tests count towards a monthly limit that depends on your plan.

## 13. Progress
**Progress** shows what you've actually done:
- Your level per skill, and how it changed over time (placement and practice evidence)
- Study time per day and week
- Exercises completed and accuracy
- Words learned and words in long-term memory
- Grammar topics mastered and topics to work on

## 14. Rewards: XP, streaks and challenges
**Rewards** turns real learning into points.

| You earn XP for | XP |
|---|---|
| Finishing an exercise | up to 10, based on your score |
| Completing a lesson | 20 |
| Remembering a flashcard | 1 |
| A tutor conversation with ≥ 4 of your turns | 15 |
| An evaluated writing task | 10 + a bonus for your level |
| Completing the placement test | 30 |

- **Streak:** a day counts when you earn XP or complete a study session of at least 5 minutes. You earn a **streak freeze** for every 7 days in a row (you can hold up to 2). A freeze covers a missed day automatically.
- **Achievements:** badges for milestones, such as your first lesson or a 30-day streak.
- **Weekly challenges:** three goals each week, with bonus XP when you complete them.
- **Leaderboard:** weekly XP rankings. These are **opt-in and off by default**. If you join, only an alias or your first name and last initial is shown.

Repeating the same exercise on the same day does not earn XP twice, and there is no XP just for opening the app.

## 15. Studying offline
You can save lessons to study without a connection (web app):
1. Open a lesson while online and choose **Save for offline**.
2. When you're offline, the app opens the **offline page**, where your saved lessons and their text exercises work. Answers are checked on your device as a preview.
3. When you're back online, your answers **sync automatically**. The server checks them again before they count towards your scores, XP and streak.

Notes:
- Audio, speaking, writing and the tutor need a connection.
- Saved lessons expire after 30 days.
- Offline answers count for the day they sync.
- Signing out removes saved lessons from the device.

## 16. The mobile app
The mobile companion app (iOS/Android) uses the same account. With it you can:
- See **today's plan**. Activities open in the web app.
- **Review flashcards**, with pinyin for Chinese.
- See your **rewards** and streak.
- Switch your **learning language** and sign out.

Your sign-in is stored securely on the device, and signing out ends it.

## 17. Plans and billing
Lessons, exercises, grammar, flashcards and the placement test (closed sections) are **free on every plan**. Paid plans give more AI practice:

| Feature | Free | Premium / Family | Pro |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tutor messages | 10 / day | 150 / day | 400 / day |
| Live voice | — | 30 min / month | 120 min / month |
| Writing evaluations | 3 / month | 40 / month | 100 / month |
| Pronunciation checks | 10 / day | 100 / day | 300 / day |
| AI-scored placement sections | 2 / month | 10 / month | 20 / month |
| Languages | 1 | 3 | unlimited |
| Exam practice tests | 1 / month | 4 / month | 10 / month |

*Limits and prices can change. **Plan & billing → Pricing** in the app always shows the current values.*

- **Upgrade:** **Plan & billing → choose a plan** opens secure checkout. Card details are handled by the payment provider, never by LinguaForge.
- **Reaching a limit:** you'll see a message saying when it resets, with a link to plans. If an AI feature fails on our side, the use is not counted.
- **Cancel or resume:** cancelling keeps your plan until the end of the paid period, and you can resume before then. Payment methods and invoices are managed in the billing portal.
- **Downgrading** never deletes progress. Languages you already have stay usable.

## 18. Family plan
The Family plan covers up to 6 people.
1. Buy the Family plan. A family group is created with you as owner.
2. **Family → Invite** by email. Invitations expire after 7 days and work only for the invited email address.
3. Members get the family plan's features while it is active.

Privacy: members see each other's **names only**. No learning data is shared within a family. If a member leaves or the plan ends, they return to their own plan, and their progress is kept.

## 19. Profile, languages and settings
**Profile & settings:**
- **Account:** name, email, native and interface language.
- **My languages:** switch the language you're learning (each has its own plan and progress), **add a language**, or **remove** one (its plan is archived).
- **Appearance:** light, dark or system theme. You can also switch between light and dark at any time with the sun/moon button at the top of every page, including the login page. The choice is remembered on that device.
- **Time zone and daily reminder.**
- **Notifications:** billing, family and learning messages appear under **Notifications**.

You can also switch the language you're learning from the language menu in the top bar.

## 20. Privacy, your data and deleting your account
- **Download my data:** request an export of everything we store about you. We email you when it's ready, and you download it from your profile.
- **Recordings:** voice recordings are used to give you feedback and are deleted automatically after a limited time.
- **Delete account:** enter your password to confirm. Your account is anonymised immediately and your learning data is permanently deleted after 30 days. This cannot be undone.

## 21. Troubleshooting and FAQ
**My level looks too low or too high.** Keep practising: your plan and mastery update as evidence builds up, and a new placement appears in your plan when it's useful.

**A listening exercise or exam section is missing.** Listening needs audio, which is generated for each piece of content. Until it exists, the exercise or section isn't offered, rather than being scored without sound.

**The microphone doesn't work.** Allow microphone access in your browser's site settings. Speaking features need HTTPS or `localhost`.

**I hit a limit.** The message tells you when it resets. Free features (lessons, exercises, flashcards) are never limited.

**My streak broke.** A streak freeze covers a missed day automatically if you have one. Check **Rewards** for your freezes.

**Offline answers didn't appear.** Reconnect and open the app: sync runs automatically. An answer the server can't accept (for example, a retired exercise) is skipped without blocking the others.

**Are exam results official?** No. They are estimates to help you prepare. Book the official exam with its provider.
